# SS Power Pros Website Skills

## Primary Website Goal
Convert local homeowners with electrical, AC, plumbing and maintenance issues into repair calls or service requests.

## Service Landing Pages
- `/services/electrical` for breaker, lighting, outlet and troubleshooting requests.
- `/services/ac` for weak cooling, tune-ups, diagnostics and equipment service requests.
- `/services/plumbing` for leaks, fixtures, water heaters, pipe and drain issues.
- `/services/maintenance` for small repairs, seasonal upkeep and punch-list work.

## Core Skills
- Electrical repair positioning for outlets, panels, lighting, faults and safer wiring.
- AC service positioning for tune-ups, repairs, cooling issues and installation requests.
- Plumbing repair positioning for leaks, fixtures, heaters and pipe issues.
- Home maintenance positioning for smaller repairs, seasonal upkeep and punch-list work.
- Fast intake messaging that moves visitors from problem recognition to call or email.
- Trust-building content through proof points, recent work, testimonials and clear process steps.

## Design Skills
- Editorial contractor landing pages with large photography, compact booking panels and strong service cards.
- Reusable component structure aligned to `components/[component-name]/[component-file].tsx`.
- CSS-token-based styling using the existing color, radius, shadow, spacing and typography variables.
- Mobile-first conversion layouts with short copy, stable controls and clear calls to action.

## Conversion Skills
- Hero sections that immediately communicate who the service is for and what action to take.
- Service-lane cards that help visitors self-identify their repair need.
- Low-friction contact paths using phone and email CTAs.
- Local-service SEO metadata and structured data for home and construction businesses.
